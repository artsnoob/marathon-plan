Key Coaching Rules (v4.5)

🛌 One true rest dayFriday = full rest. No running, strength, bouldering or padel. The single most important v4 change.
🐢 Easy means easyHR-capped at true Z2 (≤147 bpm) ≈ 7:10/km. If pace & HR disagree, obey HR and slow down.
📟 Readiness-gated quality2 consecutive POOR mornings → next quality session becomes easy. Sunday leg strength: PRIME/High full session, Low/POOR cut to 2 exercises or push to Monday.
💪 Strength 2×/week — both at homeThu = UPPER (pull-ups, dips, inverted rows, KB overhead press) + Sun = LEGS (Bulgarian split squat, single-leg RDL, KB swings, calf raises). 4 exercises each, ~25 min, 16 kg KB + Powertower. The gym session was dropped 05/08/2026 — scheduling friction was costing sessions, and adherence beats optimality. Sunday replaces the recovery jog; keep it submaximal (still ~18–24h after the long run). Never Friday. Exceptions: W14 (Sunday IS the Dam tot Dam race — Thu upper only), W15 (Sunday IS the long run), W20 (race week — no strength). Last leg session: W18 Sun (18 Oct), 14 days out; W19 = upper only.
🧗 Bouldering 1×/weekWednesday. Low-impact. First thing cut on a low-readiness week; dropped for W14 Dam tot Dam & taper.
🚶 Taper life-load tooNEAT is a training variable. Cut 20–40k-step days in peak & race weeks — a 30k-step "rest" day is not rest.
🍝 Race nutritionGel every 40–45 min. Practice on every long run. Nothing new on race day.
📉 Negative splitStart 10–15 sec/km slower than goal for the first 6 km. Run the second half faster.
🛑 Stop signs2 nights poor sleep + elevated RHR + soreness past 48 hrs → extra rest day. The plan is a guide, not a contract.
